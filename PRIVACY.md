# Privacy Policy

This application is used only by its owner, for the owner's own Google accounts.

- Data from Google APIs (Calendar, Gmail, Drive) is processed only on the owner's own computer.
- No data is collected, stored on third-party servers, sold or shared with anyone.
- OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's machine and can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
